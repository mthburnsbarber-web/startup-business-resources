# Federal, public-sector and GovRAMP resources

Official starting points for public-sector registration, funding, procurement and cloud assurance. GovRAMP and FedRAMP are separate programs. Requirements depend on the buyer, contract, data and workload; an entry here is not a claim of certification, authorization or universal applicability. The original 150-program list contains additional US and international funding tracks.
